# [DOA Estimation] Robust Direction of Arrival Estimation with Partial Gain and Phase Errors in Mobile Antenna Arrays [with MATLAB Code]

Summary: This paper addresses the problem of direction of arrival (DOA) estimation for wireless sensing systems equipped with mobile antenna arrays. To achieve high-precision DOA estimation in the presence of partial gain and phase errors (GPE), a two-stage self-calibration DOA estimation method based on multiple signal classification (MUSIC) is proposed. This method jointly estimates and compensates for GPE by first constructing a rotation-invariant signal subspace using an optimized mobile antenna array configuration, transforming the estimation of GPE into a constrained least squares problem. The closed-form expression for GPE estimation is obtained through Lagrange multiplier method. In the second stage, a denoising covariance matrix is derived, and the compensated GPE is used to complete the DOA estimation via MUSIC method. Simulation results show that compared to existing methods, the proposed self-calibration method exhibits superior DOA estimation performance when applied to mobile antenna arrays.
Introduction: Direction of arrival (DOA) estimation is a fundamental technology in array signal processing. It plays a crucial role in various radar and communication scenarios such as robot navigation and low-altitude economics, where high accuracy and resolution are required to ensure service reliability. Existing DOA estimation methods often use large or sparse antenna arrays to achieve high angle resolution beams, but these arrays have fixed antenna positions and cannot fully utilize the wireless signal variations within specific areas.
The Mobile Antenna (MA) / Fluid Antenna System, leveraging the capability of intelligent antenna positioning to dynamically optimize channel conditions, has garnered significant attention in communication and perception fields. Compared to traditional fixed antenna arrays, mobile antenna arrays allow for the antennas to be moved flexibly, fully utilizing the spatial freedom within the array area. Research has confirmed its performance advantages in multi-user communication and wireless perception. Optimized antenna layout can significantly enhance DOA estimation performance. However, existing related methods assume that the antenna array is ideally error-free, only considering noise interference on the received signal. In reality, gains and phase errors will severely degrade system performance, and the dynamic configuration of the mobile antenna array will further exacerbate such errors' impact.
Many scholars have proposed solutions to the gain and phase error issues of traditional fixed antenna arrays, mainly including iterative minimization algorithms and estimation methods based on array geometric characteristics. However, these studies focus on error estimation under a fixed antenna setup, and cannot be directly applied to mobile antenna arrays. Therefore, the urgent need is to develop robust DOA estimation algorithms suitable for mobile antenna arrays. To address the issue of DOA estimation when there are partial gain and phase errors in mobile antenna arrays, this paper proposes a two-stage self-calibration DOA estimation method based on MUSIC, achieving joint estimation and compensation of gain and phase errors, thereby enhancing the robustness and precision of DOA estimation.
Method Introduction This paper introduces a two-stage self-calibration DOA estimation algorithm based on MUSIC, which consists of two main steps:
1. Gain and phase error estimation stage
Firstly, a global optimal antenna position configuration is adopted using a movable antenna array. Subarrays with rotational invariance are selected from the array. The steering matrix of the original array is divided into two submatrices. By leveraging the linear space equivalence between the signal subspace and the steering matrix, the correlation relationship between the subarray signal subspaces is established. Subsequently, the estimation of gain and phase errors is transformed into a constrained least squares optimization problem concerning error parameters and transformation matrices. The transformation matrix is estimated using least squares method, and then the objective function and constraints are transformed to construct a Lagrangian function. Using the Lagrangian multiplier method, the closed-form estimate of the error parameters is obtained. Finally, a complete estimation of the gain and phase error matrix is further derived. To avoid singularity issues in the matrix, regularization is introduced to ensure numerical stability.

Conclusion: For the movable antenna array with partial gain phase error, this paper proposes a two-stage self-calibration DOA estimation method based on MUSIC to achieve high-precision direction of arrival (DOA) estimation. The method constructs a rotation-invariant signal subspace through an optimized array configuration, transforming the estimation of gain phase error into a constrained least squares problem, and solves it using Lagrange multipliers to obtain a closed-form solution. Error compensation is completed during the covariance matrix denoising process, followed by DOA estimation based on the MUSIC algorithm. Simulation results show that compared to traditional methods, the proposed method has higher accuracy in DOA estimation, strong robustness against phase errors, and can further improve its estimation performance with increased antenna aperture, effectively addressing the issue of DOA estimation for movable antenna arrays due to imperfect calibration.
